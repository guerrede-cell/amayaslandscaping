Frequently asked questions
All you need to know about lawn maintenance with Amaya’s Landscaping

How often should my lawn be maintained?

Most lawns benefit from weekly or bi-weekly maintenance, depending on the season and growth rate. During peak growing months, weekly service keeps your lawn looking its best.

What services are included in lawn maintenance?

Our lawn maintenance typically includes mowing, trimming, edging, and blowing off hard surfaces. We also offer additional services like fertilization, weed control, aeration, and seasonal cleanups.

Do I need to be home during service?

No, you don’t need to be home. As long as we have access to your yard, our team can complete the service and leave your property clean and well-maintained.

What happens if it rains on my service day?

If weather prevents us from servicing your lawn, we will reschedule for the next available day to ensure your lawn stays on track.

Do you offer one-time services or only recurring plans?

We offer both! Whether you need a one-time cleanup or ongoing maintenance, we can create a plan that fits your needs.
