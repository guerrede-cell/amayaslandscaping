Frequently asked questions
All you need to know about Landscaping Cleanups  with Amaya’s Landscaping

What is included in a landscaping cleanup?

Our cleanups typically include leaf removal, debris removal, trimming, weeding, edging, and blowing off all hard surfaces to leave your property neat and refreshed.

Do you offer seasonal cleanups?

Yes, we provide both spring and fall cleanups to prepare your yard for the upcoming season and keep it looking its best year-round.

How often should I schedule a cleanup?

Most homeowners schedule cleanups at least twice a year—once in the spring and once in the fall—but additional cleanups can be done as needed.

Do you remove leaves and yard waste?

Yes, we remove and properly dispose of all leaves, branches, and debris as part of our cleanup service.

What areas do you service?

We serve Bridgewater and surrounding areas throughout Somerset County, Union County, and nearby parts of New Jersey.
