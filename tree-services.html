Frequently asked questions
All you need to know about  Tree Service with Amaya’s Landscaping

What tree services do you offer?

We provide tree trimming, pruning, tree removal, stump grinding, and general tree maintenance for residential and commercial properties.

How do I know if a tree needs to be removed?

Trees that are dead, diseased, leaning, or causing damage to structures may need to be removed. We can assess your tree and recommend the best solution.

When is the best time to trim trees?

Tree trimming can be done year-round, but late fall through early spring is often ideal for most species to promote healthy growth.

Do you remove the stump after cutting a tree?

Stump removal is optional. We offer stump grinding services to fully remove the remaining stump and improve the appearance of your yard.

How much do tree services cost?

Costs vary depending on tree size, location, and complexity. We provide free estimates with clear pricing before any work begins.
