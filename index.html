Frequently asked questions
All you need to know about Amayas Landscaping

How much does landscaping cost?

Landscaping costs can vary widely depending on the size of your property, the type of work, and the materials used.

How much does lawn maintenance cost?

Lawn maintenance costs depend on your yard size, service frequency, and what’s included. On average, most homeowners spend between $100 and $400 per month for professional lawn care services.

Will I be happy with the results?

Your satisfaction is our priority. We take the time to understand your vision, preferences, and budget before starting any project, so there are no surprises along the way.

What's your landscaping process?

Our landscaping process is simple and stress-free. We start with a consultation to understand your vision, needs, and budget, followed by a site visit to evaluate your property. From there, we create a customized plan and provide a clear estimate. Once approved, our team handles the installation with attention to detail and quality. We finish with a final walkthrough to ensure you’re completely satisfied, and we also offer ongoing maintenance to keep your landscape looking its best.

What is your service area?

Our service area includes Bridgewater and surrounding areas throughout Somerset County, Union County, and nearby parts of New Jersey. We proudly serve both residential and commercial clients, delivering reliable, high-quality landscaping services. If you’re unsure whether you’re within our service area, feel free to contact us—we’re happy to help.
