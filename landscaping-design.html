Frequently asked questions
All you need to know about landscaping & design with Amaya’s Landscaping

Do you offer custom landscape design?

Yes, we create fully customized landscape designs based on your style, property, and budget to bring your vision to life.

How much does a landscaping project cost?

Costs vary depending on the size of the project, materials, and design complexity. We provide clear, detailed estimates after your consultation.

How long does a landscaping project take?

Project timelines depend on the scope of work. Smaller projects may take a few days, while larger transformations can take several weeks.

Do you help with choosing plants and materials?

Absolutely. We guide you through selecting the right plants, pavers, stone, and other materials to match your style and ensure long-lasting results.

Can you work with my existing landscape?

Yes, we can enhance, redesign, or build upon your current landscape to improve both appearance and functionality.
